=== HARDWARE MASTERY ===
> "Turn on flashlight" | "Flashlight on"
[SYS] Engages camera LED array.
> "Set brightness to maximum" | "Screen brightness 50%"
[SYS] Modifies display luminosity matrix.
> "Increase volume" | "Mute audio"
[SYS] Modifies system audio channels.
=== APP INTEGRATION ===
> "Open WhatsApp" | "Launch Spotify"
[SYS] Triggers native OS intent to launch target application.
> "Play music"
[SYS] Routes intent to default media handler (Spotify/YouTube Music).
=== TELEMETRY & NAVIGATION ===
> "What is my battery level?"
[SYS] Reads hardware battery percentage and charging state.
> "Navigate to London" | "Open maps for New York"
[SYS] Constructs deep link payload for Google Maps routing.
=== NATIVE UTILITIES ===
> "Copy to clipboard" | "Read clipboard"
[SYS] Accesses the native system clipboard for reading/writing.
> "Call John" | "Dial 1234567890"
[SYS] Launches the native dialer with the specified number.
> "Share this" | "Send to friend"
[SYS] Triggers the Android native share sheet.
> "Open google.com" | "Search for..."
[SYS] Launches URL in the default native browser.
> "Vibrate device" | "Show notification"
[SYS] Triggers haptics engine and native toast messages.

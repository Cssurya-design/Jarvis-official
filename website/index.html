Advanced AI Brain

Jarvis utilizes a Bring Your Own Key (BYOK) architecture, connecting to industry-leading AI models through Groq, Google Gemini, OpenRouter, and Pollinations AI. This gives you:

  • Groq Integration: Lightning-fast Llama 3 inference for real-time voice conversations.
  • Gemini & OpenRouter: Access to cutting-edge models like Google Gemma, Meta Llama 3.3 70B, NVIDIA Nemotron 120B, and Qwen 3.
  • Dynamic Fallback: Jarvis automatically switches providers if rate limits are reached, ensuring 100% uptime.

Vision Capabilities

Using multimodal Vision AI, Jarvis can "see" through your device's camera or analyzed images. Features include:

  • Image Analysis: Describe scenes, identify objects, and understand visual context instantly.
  • Text Extraction: Read and parse text from images seamlessly.
  • Powered by OpenRouter: Utilizes models like Llama 3.2 Vision and Nemotron-nano-vl.

Hardware Mastery

Jarvis bridges the gap between web technologies and native Android hardware using a custom Capacitor plugin bridge. Capabilities include:

  • Flashlight & Screen: Toggle the camera LED and adjust screen luminosity programmatically.
  • Audio & Haptics: Mute, raise, or lower volume, and trigger precise haptic vibration feedback.
  • Advanced Telemetry: Monitor live battery state, charging status, and detailed network health metrics.

Deep App Integration

Launch your favorite Android applications natively via URL schemes and Intent protocols. Supported integrations:

  • Communication: WhatsApp, Telegram, Gmail.
  • Entertainment: Spotify, YouTube, Netflix.
  • Social Media: Instagram, Twitter (X), Facebook.
  • Productivity: Google Chrome, Google Drive, Amazon, and Play Store.

Native Utilities

Jarvis connects with your phone's native Android utilities for a frictionless workflow. You can easily:

  • Clipboard Manager: Instantly read from or write to your system clipboard via voice.
  • Maps & Navigation: Launch Google Maps queries directly to get directions anywhere.
  • Dialer Integration: Command Jarvis to prepare phone calls by opening the dialer with a specific number.
  • Native Sharing: Share text and links seamlessly through the native Android share sheet.

Command Library

The Jarvis Command Engine doesn't rely on strict keyword matching. Instead, it uses LLM-powered natural language processing to understand your true intent. You can say:

  • "Turn on the lights" - Activates the flashlight.
  • "Play some music on Spotify" - Opens Spotify natively.
  • "Take me to the nearest coffee shop" - Launches Maps navigation.
  • "What am I looking at?" - Triggers a Vision AI analysis of your camera feed.
